Changing a Solaris hosts name

Solaris 8, 9 & 10:

Edit

/etc/hosts
/etc/hostname.hme0 (or interface)
/etc/nodename

These above files don't exist in Solaris 10 instead use:

/etc/net/ticlts/hosts
/etc/net/ticots/hosts
/etc/net/ticotsord/hosts

Changing the IP (Solaris 8 and 9):

Edit

/etc/defaultrouter
/etc/hosts
/etc/inet/netmasks

For Solaris 10 add:

/etc/inet/ipnodes
